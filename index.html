I'm a product engineer in Andorra. Over the past eight years I've built software for supermarkets, hospitals, engineering teams and schools. I'd rather own a product than work off tickets. I like sitting with the people who use what I build and fixing whatever gets in their way.

I co-founded Salut!, a retail ERP for independent supermarkets. Ten stores run on it now, and it's steady enough that it only takes an hour or two of my day. That leaves room for a remote role, either B2B or through an Andorra employer of record. If you're building something interesting, send me an email.
